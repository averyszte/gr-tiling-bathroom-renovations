## Cross-cutting SERP observations

- The Dublin SERPs for these terms are dominated by **local service pages** (LN Bathroom Renovations, New Style Bathrooms, Modern Tiling, Dublin Tiling Experts, Tony Byrne Tiling, Velvet/Galaxy/Odyssey Bathrooms) plus **cost-guide blog posts** and directories (Tradesmen.ie, Onlinetradesmen.ie, TaskMatch). A local pack (Google Business Profile map) sits above organic on every "…Dublin" and "…near me" query — so **GBP + reviews + NAP consistency are non-negotiable ranking levers**, not just the page copy.
- **Cost/price intent is the single strongest commercial-informational driver.** Nearly every category has a high-volume "cost/price in Dublin/Ireland" companion query, and the pages that win lead with concrete euro ranges, a breakdown, and cost-saving tips. A trades site that hides price loses to those that publish ranges.
- Trust signals recur everywhere: **fully insured, free quote, years of experience, reviews/testimonials, before-and-after photos, areas covered**. Gerry's "15 years, fully insured, local" positioning maps directly onto what top pages foreground.
- Strong Irish/Dublin-specific angles across all pages: **VAT at 13.5%** (reduced rate for renovation labour+materials), **older Dublin housing stock** (Victorian/1930s–70s homes → lead pipes, damaged subfloors, hidden water damage, uneven walls), **planning permission = exempted development** for standard bathrooms, **10% contingency** advice, and **"upstairs bathroom" access/waterproofing** premiums.

## 1. Bathroom renovations Dublin

**Intent:** Commercial-investigational shading into transactional. Searchers are homeowners scoping a full refit, comparing local fitters, and price-anchoring before requesting quotes. High-value lead, longer consideration cycle.

**Primary keywords:** bathroom renovation Dublin, bathroom renovations Dublin, bathroom remodelling Dublin, bathroom refurbishment Dublin, bathroom fitters Dublin.
**Long-tail:** full bathroom renovation Dublin, small bathroom renovation Dublin, luxury/ensuite bathroom renovation, bathroom renovation package deals Dublin, bathroom renovation [area — e.g. South Dublin/Dublin 4/Swords], best bathroom renovation company Dublin.

**Recurring sub-topics / FAQs top pages answer:**
- What's included in an all-inclusive package (strip-out + disposal, plumbing, electrics, plastering/wall prep, floor prep, tiling, sanitaryware, finishing).
- How long does it take? (7–10 working days standard; 10–14 for complex/wet-room/structural).
- Step-by-step process (consultation/design → strip-out → first fix → tiling → second fix → finish).
- Do I need planning permission? (No — exempted development if not altering external structure.)
- How much does it cost (see #6) and how to save (keep the existing layout).

**Dublin angles:** older housing stock surprises (contingency 10%), moving toilet/shower/sink adds €1,800–€3,000+, VAT 13.5%, area-served coverage across Dublin city + commuter towns.

## 2. Tiling services Dublin (floor / wall / bathroom / kitchen / splashbacks)

**Intent:** Mixed. Some want tiling *as a standalone trade* (supply + fit, or fit-only of client's tiles); others land here mid-renovation. Slightly more transactional/"hire a tiler" than #1. "Tiler Dublin" and "tiler near me" are the money terms.

**Primary keywords:** tiler Dublin, tiling services Dublin, tiling contractors Dublin, tiler near me, professional tiling Dublin.
**Long-tail / sub-service (each a candidate sibling page):** floor tiling Dublin, wall tiling Dublin, bathroom tiling Dublin, kitchen tiling Dublin, kitchen splashback tiling Dublin, large-format tile installation, mosaic tiling, hallway/porch tiling, outdoor/patio tiling, herringbone/chevron tile patterns, tile supply and fit vs fit-only.

**Recurring sub-topics / FAQs:**
- Do you supply tiles or fit customer-supplied tiles? (Both models appear.)
- Price per m² and per-job (splashback ≈ €250–€500; see #6 for m² rates).
- Tile-size and pattern effect on price (large-format, mosaic, herringbone/chevron +40–50% labour).
- Residential vs commercial; renovations, restoration and tiled-floor cleaning as add-ons.
- Waterproofing/wall prep before tiling; substrate levelling.

**Dublin angles:** Dublin labour ~10–20% above rest of Ireland; upstairs-bathroom access & waterproofing premiums; period-home uneven walls needing prep.

**Overlap note:** "Bathroom tiling Dublin" straddles #1, #2 and #4. Differentiate: the **tiling** page = the trade/craft, tile types, patterns, per-m² pricing, fit-only option; the **bathroom renovation** page = full turnkey refit. Cross-link, don't duplicate.

## 3. Tile repairs / regrouting / silicone renewal / cracked & loose tiles

**Intent:** Problem-aware, often urgent, **lower ticket / faster-converting**. Homeowner has a specific failure (mouldy grout, leaking silicone, one cracked or drummy tile, water getting behind tiles) and wants a fix without a full rip-out. Strong "near me" + "today/urgent" flavour. Excellent entry-level lead that can upsell to renovation.

**Primary keywords:** tile regrouting Dublin, regrouting Dublin, grout repair Dublin, silicone replacement/renewal Dublin, tile repair Dublin.
**Long-tail:** cracked tile repair Dublin, loose/drummy tile repair, replace broken bathroom tile, shower regrouting, mouldy/black grout removal, bathroom silicone reseal, regrout vs retile, tile & grout cleaning/restoration.

**Recurring sub-topics / FAQs:**
- When should you regrout? (Every ~8–10 years; sooner if cracked/chipped to prevent water damage.)
- Regrout vs replace tiles vs full retile — cost/decision framing.
- Why it matters: failed grout/silicone → water ingress behind tiles → far costlier damage.
- Can grout be replaced without removing tiles? (Yes — cut out old grout, reapply.)
- Cost: grout/silicone work ≈ €18/m² labour+materials (budget category vs full renovation).
- Mould, staining, water leaking through, "tiles sound hollow."

**Dublin angles:** older bathrooms with dated/failing seals; damp/mould in period homes; positioned as the affordable "refresh without renovating" option — a distinct, high-conversion sibling page.

## 4. Wet room installation Dublin

**Intent:** Commercial-investigational, higher spec/ticket than a standard bathroom. Searchers weigh **wet room vs walk-in shower** and worry about waterproofing done right. Overlaps heavily with accessible bathrooms (#5) but the wet-room searcher is often design/lifestyle-led, not mobility-led.

**Primary keywords:** wet room installation Dublin, wet room Dublin, wet room conversion Dublin.
**Long-tail:** wet room cost Dublin, wet room vs walk-in shower, tanking/waterproofing a wet room, level-access shower Dublin, ensuite wet room, small wet room ideas.

**Recurring sub-topics / FAQs:**
- Wet room vs walk-in shower — cost & suitability (wet room ≈ €2,000–€4,000 more; drain, graded screed, full tanking).
- Tanking/waterproofing explained (materials+labour €300–€800; failure could cost €3,000–€8,000 to fix) — the trust/expertise hook.
- Drainage & floor gradient/screed; slip-resistant tiles.
- Cost ranges (installation from ~€7,000; full high-spec €15,000–€22,000+).
- Will it leak / how do you guarantee waterproofing? (biggest anxiety — answer it explicitly.)

**Dublin angles:** suitability of older Dublin floor structures for level access, VAT 13.5%, upstairs waterproofing risk.

**Overlap note:** vs #5 — wet room page = design/lifestyle + waterproofing craft; accessible page = mobility, safety, grants. Both mention level-access; keep the *reason to install* distinct.

## 5. Accessible / mobility bathrooms Dublin

**Intent:** Needs-driven, often researched by **adult children or carers** for an elderly/disabled parent, or by people planning to age in place. Emotional + practical. Very strong **grant/funding** sub-intent that most competitors lead with — this is the defining differentiator of the category.

**Primary keywords:** mobility bathrooms Dublin, accessible bathroom Dublin, disabled bathroom Dublin, walk-in shower for elderly Dublin.
**Long-tail:** level-access shower room Dublin, bathroom adaptations for elderly, disabled wet room grant Ireland, half-height shower door, grab rails / non-slip flooring installation, ageing-in-place bathroom.

**Recurring sub-topics / FAQs:**
- **Grants** (the headline): Housing Adaptation Grant for People with a Disability (up to ~95% of cost), Mobility Aids Grant Scheme, Housing Aid for Older Persons Scheme — and help completing council/HSE paperwork and meeting grant design requirements.
- Accessibility features: walk-in shower/wet room, level entry, grab bars, non-slip flooring, height-adjustable/wall-mounted sink, half-height doors, seats.
- Timeline (shower-room conversions ~3–5 days).
- Approved-contractor / HSE-liaison reassurance.

**Dublin angles:** local council grant administration (Dublin City / Fingal / DLR / South Dublin), approved-supplier status, insured tradesman doing grant-compliant work.

**Overlap note:** shares walk-in-shower and wet-room vocabulary with #4 — this page owns *grants, safety, mobility*; #4 owns *design + tanking*.

## 6. Bathroom renovation cost Dublin

**Intent:** Highest-volume informational-commercial query in the cluster; **the price-anchoring hub** feeding every other page. Searchers want real euro numbers, a breakdown, and "what drives the price." Winning pages are transparent, structured, and end with a soft CTA to a free quote.

**Primary keywords:** bathroom renovation cost Dublin, how much does a bathroom renovation cost Dublin/Ireland, bathroom renovation price Dublin.
**Long-tail:** small bathroom renovation cost, cost to tile a bathroom Ireland, tiler cost per square metre Ireland, cost of a wet room Ireland, walk-in shower cost Ireland, bathroom renovation cost calculator/breakdown.

**Reference figures to anchor content (2025–26, Dublin):**
- Full standard bathroom refit: ~€9,000–€15,000; basic refresh €5,000–€7,000; luxury €18,000+.
- Labour ≈ 40–50% of budget; add 10% contingency.
- Moving toilet/shower/sink: +€1,800–€3,000.
- Tiling labour ≈ €30–€50/m² (Dublin +10–20%); patterns +40–50%; splashback €250–€500.
- Regrout/silicone ≈ €18/m². Wet room premium +€2,000–€4,000; tanking €300–€800.
- VAT 13.5% on renovation labour+materials.

**FAQs:** What's included; why quotes vary; how to save money; are hidden costs common (older Dublin homes); is VAT included.

**Overlap note:** cost content lives *everywhere*, but this is the pillar. Give each service page its own concise price block and link up to this hub — avoid one giant duplicated price table across the site.
