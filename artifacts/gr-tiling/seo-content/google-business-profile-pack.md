## 4. Reviews — the highest-impact ongoing task

The research is clear: the local pack rewards **review volume, recency and keyword-rich content**. You currently have three reviews; the site can only feature those three across ten pages. **Getting more reviews that name the specific job** is the single most valuable thing you can do — it also lets us map reviews 1:1 onto pages later (wet room, splashback, regrout, accessible).

### Ask after every job (SMS/WhatsApp template)
> Hi [name], thanks again for having me in to do your [wet room / kitchen splashback / bathroom regrout / accessible bathroom]. If you were happy with it, a quick Google review would mean a lot — and if you can mention the actual job (e.g. "[wet room in Raheny]"), even better. Here's the link: [your Google review short link]. Cheers, Gerry.

**Prioritise asking** customers whose job was a **wet room, accessible/grant bathroom, kitchen splashback, or grout repair** — those are the four pages currently leaning on borrowed quotes.

### Reply to every review (templates — replies are a ranking signal)
- **5-star:** "Thanks a million, [name] — it was a pleasure doing your [job] in [area]. Enjoy it, and shout if you ever need anything. — Gerry"
- **Critical/negative:** "Thanks for the feedback, [name]. That's not the standard I hold myself to — I'd like to make it right. Can you call me on +353 87 720 9850 so we can sort it? — Gerry" *(always calm, never defensive, take it offline.)*
