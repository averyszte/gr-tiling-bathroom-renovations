## OPTIONAL DEEP SECTION

### Heading: Why "Fully Insured" and "Time-Served" Actually Matter

Anyone can call themselves a tiler — there's no mandatory licence to hang a shingle in Ireland. That's precisely why the words "fully insured" and "fifteen years" carry weight, and why it's worth understanding what they protect you from.

Insurance matters because bathroom work touches things that go badly wrong when they're done poorly: water, electrics, and structure. A shower that isn't tanked correctly can leak into the room below and cause serious, expensive damage months after the tiler has gone. Proper cover means that if the worst happens, you're not left carrying it alone. Uninsured "cash job" trades are cheaper for a reason — the risk sits entirely with you. Gerry carries insurance on every job, and that's a deliberate choice about how the business treats its customers.

The fifteen years matter because good tiling and bathroom work is almost entirely about the parts you never see. The waterproofing behind the tile. The floor stiffened and decoupled so a bouncing Dublin joist doesn't crack the grout. The movement joints, the primer, the timber pattress behind the wall so a grab rail or heavy mirror has something solid to fix into. A good finish can hide a bad build for a while — but only experience gets those hidden details right first time. That's the value of a time-served tradesman over the lowest quote, and it's what stands behind everything GR Tiling & Bathroom Renovations puts its name to.
